Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Tailwind ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address. During onboarding you provide your airline crew portal (Okta/HYPR) username. Your profile photo is optional.

When you sync your roster, Tailwind downloads your roster PDF from your airline's crew portal on your device and sends it to our server for parsing. Your employment ID is extracted from the PDF and saved to your profile. Flight and duty information extracted from the PDF is stored on your device.

If you enable the optional calendar sync feature, Tailwind accesses your device calendar to write your roster as calendar events.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Tailwind service — displaying your roster, duties, and flight information in the app. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your profile (name, email, employment ID) is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database. Your profile photo, if provided, is stored in Supabase Storage.

Your parsed roster data is stored locally on your device. If you use the friend roster sharing feature, your roster is also uploaded to Supabase so accepted friends can access it — this data is protected by row-level security and is only visible to users you have explicitly accepted.

When you sync, your roster PDF is sent to our server for parsing. During this early development phase of the app, we temporarily retain a copy of the uploaded PDF and the resulting parsed flight data for up to 14 days, solely to diagnose and fix parsing or display bugs when you report an issue. This debug data is stored in a restricted database table that is never accessible to other users and cannot be reached through the app itself — only we, as the developers, can access it directly, and it is automatically and permanently deleted after the 14-day window. This is a temporary practice: once roster parsing has stabilized, we will stop retaining PDFs and parsed data altogether and this section will be updated to reflect that.

Your crew portal username is stored exclusively in your device's encrypted secure storage and is never saved on our servers.

4. Your Crew Portal Credentials

Your airline crew portal password is never collected or stored by Tailwind. Your username is stored in your device's encrypted secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and is only used locally during the sync process — it is not transmitted to or retained by our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Tailwind uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (authentication, database, and file storage)
  • Google (sign-in)
  • Apple (sign-in)

If you enable calendar sync, your roster events are written directly to your chosen device calendar provider (e.g. Apple Calendar, Google Calendar). We do not share your personal data with any other third parties.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you have an active account. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Tailwind is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: tailwind.support@gmail.com
